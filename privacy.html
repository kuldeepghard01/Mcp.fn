Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2026

This policy explains what information MCP Fantasy ("we", "us", operated by Kuldeep) collects, how we use it and what choices you have. By using the app you agree to this policy.

1. Information we collect

  • Account details: your name, email address, phone number and password. Your password is handled by our sign-in provider and stored in a hashed form. We cannot see it.
  • Game data: your predictions, answers, scores, ranks, coin balance and coin history, referral code and the friends you referred, and details you enter when you request a reward (movie, city and country, contact detail).
  • Usage data: when you open the app, the number of ads you watch, and your last active time. We use this to run the game, prevent abuse and count active users.
  • Advertising data: our ad partner Google AdMob may collect your device advertising ID, IP address and device information to show and measure ads.

2. How we use your information

  • To create and run your account and the contests.
  • To calculate scores, rank players, give coins and handle reward requests.
  • To prevent cheating, fake accounts and misuse.
  • To show ads and measure how the app is used.
  • To answer your questions and meet legal duties.

3. Who we share it with

We do not sell your personal information. We use these service providers, who process data for us:

  • Supabase for our database and sign-in. Our data is stored in a data centre in Sydney, Australia.
  • Expo (EAS) to deliver app updates.
  • Google AdMob for advertising, and YouTube to play movie trailers inside the app.

Other players only see your display name on leaderboards. Our team can see your name, email and phone number to run contests and give rewards.

4. Ads and your choices

Ads are shown by Google AdMob. Where the law requires it, we ask for your consent. You can reset or delete your advertising ID or turn off ad personalization in your phone settings.

5. How long we keep data

We keep your account and game data until you delete your account. Old activity statistics are removed after about 45 to 90 days.

6. Your rights

You can ask us to see, correct or delete your data. You can delete your account yourself in the app: Profile → Delete my account. See how to delete your account. You can also email us at kuldeepgurjar90016@gmail.com.

7. Children

MCP Fantasy is only for people aged 18 or older. We do not knowingly collect information from children.

8. Security

We use encrypted connections (HTTPS) and access rules so that players can only see their own private data. No system is completely secure, so we cannot promise absolute security.

9. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy. The date at the top shows the latest version.
